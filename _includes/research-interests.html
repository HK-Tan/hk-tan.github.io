My research is now focused on problems in AI safety. My current research interests include: 🤝 Multi-Agent Safety & Interpretability: - Detecting and understanding collusion between AI agents (e.g. Schelling point coordination and tacit collusion). - Applying techniques from game theory and decision theory. - Adapting single-agent black-box and white-box monitoring methods to multi-agent systems. 🔬 Applied White-Box Methods & Model Organisms: - Monitoring models through their internals as a stand-in for chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring as CoT becomes less faithful. - Building model organisms to evaluate the effectiveness of black-box and white-box methods. - Developing and applying nonlinear/geometric methods for interpretability (drawing on neural geometry, Riemannian geometry, and differential topology). 🔭 Scalable Oversight via Interpretability: - Developing foundational interpretability models/agents that can audit other models at scale, as a white-box counterpart to black-box protocols such as debate. These have evolved from my earlier work in: - 📊 Statistics: Causality/Causal Inference/Causal Machine Learning and High-Dimensional Statistics. - 📐 Mathematics: Hyperbolic PDEs and Conservation Laws. - ⚛️ Physics: Nuclear Astrophysics, Mathematical General Relativity. Throughout, I think of myself as a "full stack mathematician" — I take projects end-to-end, from theory and modeling through implementation and simulation to production-scale systems whenever possible!
